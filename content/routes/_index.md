{{< lead >}}
A log of hikes, runs and rides, each mapped from its GPX track.
{{< /lead >}}

To create a route I just upload a `.gpx` or `.fit` file to the `content/routes/`
folder; a new card then appears automatically once the site rebuilds. (`.fit` —
the native Coros/Garmin export — is much smaller than GPX.)

Remember to name files as `YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.gpx` (or `.fit`) to keep them in chronological order and ensure each route has a date and title even when the track itself carries none.
