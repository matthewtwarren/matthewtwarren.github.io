To create a route just upload a `.fit` or `.gpx` file to the `content/routes/`
folder; a new card then appears automatically once the site rebuilds.

Remember to name files as `YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.gpx` (or `.fit`) to keep them in chronological order and ensure each route has a date and title even when the track itself carries none.

Note that `.fit` files are the native format to COROS/Garmin devices, and are preferred over `.gpx` files as they are typically much smaller in size.
